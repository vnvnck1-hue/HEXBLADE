Keep broad positions from Image 1 but redesign the shells and sizes. Lower-right: retain the familiar OPEN CIRCULAR skill arrangement from Image 1, with the largest red sword circle at far lower-right, rifle immediately left, boots slightly left of rifle; booster/energy/missile above, E as tiny outer-left circle. Panel trims are narrow controlled cream cuts, not thick giant torn sheets. Sparse lime corner streaks ONLY at ready markers. Keep the five UI regions balanced, with much smaller portraits than source. Crisp production-ready layout.
