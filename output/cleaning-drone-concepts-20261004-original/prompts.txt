01_kite_sweeper
Use case: stylized-concept. Create one highly ORIGINAL, adorable, playful SD manned cleaning drone for a sci-fi action game.
Art direction: charm and bold exaggerated shape language reminiscent of Brawl Stars game characters: toy-like chunky proportions, huge dominant shapes, tiny supports, bright confident color blocking, thick soft bevels, simple broad highlights and colorful shadows, expressive lively pose. A polished clean 3D-cartoon-like game concept illustration, not a realistic gritty machine, not industrial engineering illustration. NO copied Brawl Stars character, costume, emblem or exact existing design. Fresh silhouette takes priority.
Female teammate rides concealed inside opaque shell, slightly smaller than main player's mecha. Show a subtle closed boarding hatch, never human anatomy or transparent cockpit. Simplify engineering to support a joyful characterful vehicle silhouette.
CRITICAL: NO FACE of any kind. No eyes, face display, dark visor, paired eye-like lights, smile, mouth arrangement or emoji. Personality from proportions, asymmetry, jaunty stance and shapes only. Front is painted opaque shell. No pseudo-face using vents and lamps.
Do not use conventional tank, rice cooker, cylindrical pot, spherical pet robot, ring drone, realistic excavator, ordinary car or literal household vacuum shape. Avoid reference imitation: build from the specific NEW silhouette below.
One dominant cabin mass, one iconic cleaning feature, only a few supporting parts. No panel clutter, exposed pistons, tangled hoses, gun barrels, grime or scratches. Visible cleaning action is light and secondary. One machine only in one full-body three-quarter view on pale warm gray background, simple soft contact shadow, fully in frame, no text, no logos, no diagram.
Design 1: a PLUMP FOLDED-KITE CLEANUP HOPPER. Main enclosed cabin is a broad rounded diamond / paper-kite shape, with a high chunky rear hump tapering to a short blunt downward-curved prow. Silhouette has strong diagonal swept shapes, like a lively hopping manta made of folded cushioned blocks, NOT an aircraft with long wings. Two thick short folded triangular side flaps create a jaunty uneven pose: left raised slightly and right lowered. Two tiny chunky boot-like paddle feet beneath back corners support it. ONE oversized thick crescent-shaped flexible sweeping brush extends UNDER the prow, hugging the floor, like a squashy curved paintbrush that sweeps three small scraps sideways. Brush is a single bright chunky feature, no suction mouth on face. Boarding hatch a diagonal seam on rear hump. Saturated but tasteful peach-coral main shell, cream diagonal band, vivid violet short feet, golden yellow brush. Broad opaque front completely blank. Playful quick energetic personality, tilted slightly forward like it is hopping into a sweep. Minimal rounded geometry, no literal paper texture or facial markings.
